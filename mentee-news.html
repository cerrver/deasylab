How this feed is built. The feed shows items from the last 24 months, newest first (older items stay available via "Show older items"). Once a week an automated check searches for new items about each mentee in the roster below: papers in leading journals (the Nature, Science, Cell, Lancet, NEJM and JAMA families, and the field's top journals), press and media coverage, and major awards, grants, or appointments. Each candidate must resolve to a direct link before it is added, and the lab reviews the additions before they are published. Items marked verify carry a detail that is still being confirmed.
